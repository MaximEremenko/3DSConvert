3DSConvert

Converts 3-D diffuse scattering data between the unified HDF5 format (/scattering/data and /entry/data layouts, RMCProfile / DiffuseCode), Yell 1.0 HDF5 (DISCUS / Yell / Meerkat), the RMCProfile old text format, and VTK (Scatty / Spinteract / 3DSCalculator), and reads NeXus volumes (Mantid MDHistoWorkspace, NXdata such as NXrefine). On the way it can crop, rebin, mask, subtract backgrounds, symmetrize and compute the 3D-ΔPDF. Everything runs locally in your browser — no upload.
